WATCHMAN THROUGH THE CAMERAS (optional, version {{VERSION}})

Argus can listen through the microphone of the cameras you choose and answer
through their speaker when someone calls it by name, like a call. It is off
by default. Accepting it here does not turn it on: you still switch it on in
the app (People > Household privacy) and on each camera.

By turning it on, you accept that:
- You, who install and operate Argus, are responsible for using it lawfully
  in {{COUNTRY}} ({{LAWS}}). Camera audio counts as video surveillance data.
- You must place visible signs at every access to an area with listening
  cameras.
- You must not enable audio in the rest areas of domestic workers: mark
  those cameras as "no audio".
- Recognising people who do not live in the house by face or voice needs
  their consent, in writing for biometric data.
- Argus keeps no audio before someone calls it by name; only the
  conversation is kept, with the same retention as voice calls. Every
  conversation starts with a tone, and everything runs on your hardware.
- Each member of the household can ask that their location and their voice
  or face recognition are not used in these conversations.
