RECOGNIZE RECURRING VISITORS (optional, version {{VERSION}})

Argus can learn the faces of outside people who often pass your cameras
(neighbours, couriers, visitors) to recognize them as recurring instead of
always treating them as strangers. It is off by default. Accepting it here
does not turn it on: you still enable it from the app (People > Household
privacy).

By turning it on you accept that:
- Argus will process the faces of outside people to recognize them as
  recurring. This is third parties' biometric data.
- You must put up visible camera signs.
- Retention is limited: an unnamed visitor is erased after 30 days unseen
  (configurable from 1 to 60); turning the feature off erases unnamed
  visitors within hours. Only small face crops are kept, never video, and
  they are never synced to phones.
